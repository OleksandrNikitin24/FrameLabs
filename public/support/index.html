Support & FAQ

Information for FlowCut users, account access, licensing, and privacy requests.

What application does FlowCut support?

FlowCut is designed for Final Cut Pro workflows and exports sequence XML for use in Final Cut Pro.

Where do I edit my account information?

Sign in and select the account icon. Optional address and contact details can be edited under My Account.

Where will products or invoices appear?

Purchased or downloaded products, subscriptions, and invoices will appear in the matching account sections once a completed transaction has been recorded.

Can I enable two-step authentication?

Yes. Signed-in users can set up optional two-step authentication with an authenticator app from My Account.

How do I make a privacy request?

Review the GDPR Portal and submit the request through Contact Us.
