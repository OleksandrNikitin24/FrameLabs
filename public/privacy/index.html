Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 24, 2026

FrameLabs provides the FlowCut website, account system, and software products. This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is collected, processed, and protected when you use FrameLabs services, create an account, purchase products, or interact with the website.

FrameLabs operates from Germany.

By using FrameLabs services, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information you provide

When you create an account, purchase a product, or contact FrameLabs, certain information may be processed, including:

  • Email address
  • Authentication information
  • Account profile information
  • Billing-related identifiers
  • Optional personal details you choose to provide

Optional profile information may include:

  • First name
  • Last name
  • Country
  • Address
  • Postcode
  • Phone number

Two-factor authentication may be enabled voluntarily through supported authentication applications.

2. Authentication providers

FrameLabs supports third-party authentication providers including:

  • Apple Sign In
  • Google Sign In

When you authenticate through these services, certain account information may be shared with FrameLabs in accordance with the provider's own privacy policies and authorization settings.

3. Payments and billing

Payments, subscriptions, and license transactions may be processed through third-party payment providers including:

  • Stripe
  • LemonSqueezy

FrameLabs does not directly store full payment card information on its own servers.

Payment providers process billing information according to their own privacy and compliance obligations.

4. Media processing

FlowCut primarily processes audio, video, and timeline-related media locally on the user's device.

FrameLabs does not permanently upload or store user media files unless explicitly required for future cloud-based functionality or optional online services.

Users remain responsible for maintaining backups of:

  • Project files
  • Media assets
  • Exports
  • Editing timelines

5. AI-assisted features

Certain FlowCut features may use AI-assisted processing technologies, including:

  • Speech-to-text
  • Silence detection
  • Filler-word detection
  • Editing automation
  • Transcription systems

Where possible, processing is performed locally on the user's device.

Future cloud-based AI functionality may involve temporary server-side processing, which will be disclosed separately if introduced.

6. Analytics and cookies

FrameLabs may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Maintain authentication sessions
  • Improve website functionality
  • Analyze product usage
  • Monitor performance
  • Enhance user experience

Analytics services may include Google Analytics.

These technologies may collect information such as:

  • Browser type
  • Device information
  • Approximate location
  • Usage behavior
  • Referring pages
  • Session activity

Users may control certain cookie settings through their browser preferences.

7. How information is used

FrameLabs may process information to:

  • Create and manage accounts
  • Authenticate users
  • Deliver purchased licenses and services
  • Operate billing systems
  • Provide support
  • Improve products and features
  • Maintain platform security
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Prevent fraud or abuse

8. Service providers and infrastructure

FrameLabs may use third-party providers to operate services related to:

  • Authentication
  • Hosting
  • Analytics
  • Billing
  • Infrastructure
  • Customer support

Current providers may include:

  • Supabase
  • Google Analytics
  • Stripe
  • LemonSqueezy
  • Apple authentication services
  • Google authentication services
  • Resend for contact form email delivery

Service providers process information only as necessary to deliver their services and under applicable contractual and privacy obligations.

9. Data retention

FrameLabs retains personal information only for as long as necessary to:

  • Operate services
  • Maintain accounts
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Resolve disputes
  • Enforce agreements

Users may request deletion of eligible personal information through the GDPR Portal or support contact methods.

Certain transactional, billing, fraud-prevention, or legally required records may be retained where required by law.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Export eligible data
  • Request deletion
  • Object to certain processing
  • Restrict processing activities

Users located in the European Union may have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Privacy requests may be submitted through the GDPR Portal, account settings, support contact methods, or direct email communication.

11. Security

FrameLabs uses commercially reasonable security measures designed to protect personal information and account access.

However, no online system, transmission method, or storage platform can be guaranteed completely secure.

Users are responsible for maintaining secure passwords and protecting account credentials.

12. Children's privacy

FrameLabs services are not directed toward children under the age required by applicable law.

FrameLabs does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

13. Changes to this policy

FrameLabs may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect product changes, legal requirements, infrastructure updates, or feature additions.

Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
