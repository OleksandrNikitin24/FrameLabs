Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 24, 2026

1. About this policy

FrameLabs provides the FlowCut website and product experience. This policy explains the information processed when you browse the site, create an account, or use account features.

2. Information you provide

When you register or sign in, we process your email address and authentication information. If you choose to complete your account profile, we process optional information such as your first name, last name, address, postcode, city, country, and phone number.

If you contact FrameLabs, we process the name, email address, inquiry category, and message you submit so we can receive and respond to your request.

Two-step authentication can be enabled voluntarily through an authenticator application.

3. How information is used

  • To create, authenticate, and protect your FrameLabs account.
  • To allow you to manage optional account details.
  • To provide support and respond to privacy requests.
  • To operate product access, billing, or license services when those features are made available.

4. Service providers

FrameLabs uses Supabase to provide account authentication, account storage, and contact submission processing. FrameLabs uses Resend to deliver contact form messages by email. Service providers process information only as needed to deliver their services and according to their applicable terms and privacy obligations.

5. Your choices and rights

You can update optional personal details from your signed-in account page. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or request deletion of personal information. See the GDPR Portal for request guidance.

6. Contact

Privacy questions and requests can be initiated from the Contact Us page.
