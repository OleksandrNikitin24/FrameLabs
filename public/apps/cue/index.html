Script library

Every script, right where you left it.

Create as many scripts as you need. Cue saves them locally and remembers your reading position, so you can pick up mid-page.

  • Search and sort by title, creation date, or modification date.
  • Rename, duplicate, and delete from your library.
  • Edit with confidence. Undo and redo, copy and paste, and protection for unsaved edits.
  • Stored on your device. No account, no sync server.

Flexible scrolling

Scroll at your own pace.

Set the speed, press play, and Cue does the rest. Grab the script whenever you want to take over.

  • Play, pause, restart. Jump to the beginning or end, or step forward and backward.
  • Time and pace. See remaining reading time and estimated words per minute.
  • Countdown. An optional countdown before playback begins.
  • Tap anywhere on the script to toggle playback.
  • Drag override. Scroll by hand during playback when you want to.
  • Clean view. Hide the controls while you read.

Customize the reading experience

Make it read the way you speak.

Adjust the display to match your setup, in a dark, high-contrast reading environment.

  • Type. Text size, line spacing, and multiple fonts.
  • Color. Choose your own text and background colors.
  • Alignment. Left, center, right, or formatted.
  • Reading focus. Set where on the screen your eyes land.
  • Mirror. Flip the text for beam-splitter or teleprompter glass, on the device, an external display, or both.

Voice Follow

Speak. Cue keeps your place.

Voice Follow listens to your speech and advances the script as you read. No tapping, no racing the scroll.

  • Handles pauses naturally, and holds while you think.
  • Forgiving. Tolerates skipped or repeated words and recovers after small recognition errors.
  • Multilingual text matching, with supported languages and offline models shown on your device.
  • On-device first. Online recognition is used only after you approve it.

Cue does not save microphone recordings or create a permanent transcript history. Try Voice Follow free with an English sample script; Cue Pro unlocks it for your own scripts.
