Privacy Rights

GDPR Portal

Use this page to understand and manage the personal-data rights available to FrameLabs users. Requests are verified before account information, exports, or deletion actions are processed.

FrameLabs operates from Germany and processes requests in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Access or portability

Request confirmation of whether FrameLabs processes personal information associated with your account. Eligible users may also request a copy of their personal data in a portable format.

Exportable information may include:

  • Account information
  • Profile information
  • Authentication records
  • Connected login providers
  • Billing identifiers
  • License information

Media files processed through FlowCut are handled locally on the user's device and are not uploaded to FrameLabs servers unless explicitly stated for future cloud-based features.

2. Correction

You may update optional profile information directly from your account settings when available.

If corrections cannot be completed through your account dashboard, you may submit a verified request to modify inaccurate or incomplete personal information.

3. Deletion

You may request permanent deletion of your FrameLabs account and eligible associated personal information.

Approved deletion requests may remove:

  • Account records
  • Authentication information
  • Connected providers
  • Profile information
  • Stored license associations

FrameLabs does not permanently store user media files processed locally through FlowCut.

Some transactional, billing, fraud-prevention, or legally required records may be temporarily retained where required by applicable law or payment-provider obligations.

4. Object or restrict processing

You may request restriction of certain forms of data processing or object to specific processing activities where applicable under GDPR regulations.

This may include:

  • Analytics-related processing
  • Marketing communications
  • Optional account features
  • Certain AI-assisted functionality

5. Authentication and third-party providers

FrameLabs may use third-party providers to operate account authentication, billing, analytics, and infrastructure services.

Current providers may include:

  • Apple Sign In
  • Google Sign In
  • Supabase Authentication
  • Stripe
  • LemonSqueezy
  • Google Analytics

These providers process information only as required to deliver their respective services and under their own privacy and compliance obligations.

6. AI-powered features

Certain FlowCut features may use AI-assisted processing technologies, including:

  • Speech-to-text
  • Silence detection
  • Filler-word detection
  • Editing automation

Where possible, media processing is performed locally on the user's device. Future cloud-based AI features will be disclosed separately if introduced.

Users remain responsible for reviewing generated edits, exports, and automated processing results before publication or commercial use.

7. Verification process

To protect account security and user privacy, FrameLabs may require identity verification before processing certain GDPR-related requests.

Verification may include:

  • Authenticated account access
  • Email confirmation
  • Ownership verification of connected accounts or licenses

Requests that cannot be verified may be denied for security reasons.

8. Request processing time

Verified GDPR requests are typically reviewed and processed within 30 days, depending on request complexity and applicable legal obligations.

9. Submit a privacy request

You may submit GDPR-related requests through the authenticated account dashboard, the contact form, or direct email communication.
